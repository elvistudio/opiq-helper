## 4. Teacher-review procedure

## 5. Local-safety-review procedure

The artifact itself is a paper-only activity and declares no local practical risk assessment. Before a classroom trial, a separate named-context delivery review must still confirm one named school, exact classroom, date, group size and supervision, the paper-material-only boundary, absence of flame, chemicals, inhalation or gas collection, accessibility and stop signals.

## 6. Finding severity definitions

`note` records context; `minor` requires a bounded correction; `major` blocks a positive decision until resolved; `blocking` prevents use. Positive decisions cannot hide open major or blocking findings.

## 7. Approval rules

A template cannot approve anything. Completed records require real reviewer identity, role, date, full scope, current fingerprint and explicit decision. Named-context approval never transfers automatically to another site, date or group.

## 8. Fingerprint invalidation

Any material-byte change changes a material hash and aggregate fingerprint. Old review evidence then becomes stale and cannot support readiness.

## 9. Required-change workflow

Record each required change, update only the affected material, recompute hashes and fingerprint, and obtain review of the new bytes. Do not rewrite historical completed evidence.

## 10. Classroom-trial boundary

No trial may begin until a current approved teacher record and current named-context local-delivery approval exist. A classroom-trial template is workflow infrastructure, not evidence.

## 11. Prohibited claims

Do not commit learner identities. Do not claim that the paper model measures classroom air, air quality, room safety, a current exact carbon-dioxide concentration, official curriculum completeness, publication readiness or effectiveness. Lesson 51 remains `missing`.

## 12. How to create a completed record

Copy the relevant template to a new path inside this review directory, set `template: false`, provide genuine reviewer data, complete every scope item and register the path. Never infer a review from PR authorship, approval or merge state.
