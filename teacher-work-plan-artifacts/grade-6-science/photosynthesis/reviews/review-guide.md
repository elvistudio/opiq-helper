# Human-review guide: Grade 6 photosynthesis artifact

## 1. Purpose and boundaries

This packet supports a future subject/method review and a separate named-context safety review. Templates are not human evidence. Merging a pull request is neither teacher approval nor safety approval, and no canonical source gap is resolved.

## 3. Files under review

Review the artifact index and five Markdown materials: practical protocol, observation table, student worksheet, answer key and assessment rubric. The review templates and trial workflow describe evidence capture; they are not teaching evidence.

## 4. Teacher-review procedure

Copy the teacher template to a new completed-record path only after a real reviewer supplies a stable ID, name, role and date. Review every declared area, including scientific accuracy, measurement validity, Russian explanation, Estonian support and readiness boundaries. Record findings and affected paths precisely.

## 5. Local-safety-review procedure

Local approval is limited to one named school, exact classroom or laboratory, activity date, group size, adult supervision, `indoor_classroom` delivery category, accessibility arrangements and emergency process. Review electrical/spill separation, cool-lamp temperature and distance, container stability, solution handling, allergy risk, cleanup and non-release of aquarium plants. Approval in one context is not universal safety or permission elsewhere.

## 6. Finding severity definitions

- `note`: useful observation with no required change.
- `minor`: bounded change that does not block safe internal review.
- `major`: substantial scientific, instructional or feasibility problem.
- `blocking`: prevents safe or valid use.

## 7. Approval rules

Templates must remain pending. A completed positive decision requires the current fingerprint, every scope area reviewed, required changes complete and no open major or blocking finding. Conditional safety approval must state every condition explicitly.

## 8. Fingerprint invalidation

Any material-byte change creates new hashes and a new aggregate fingerprint. Earlier completed review evidence then becomes stale and cannot support readiness until the changed bytes are reviewed again.

## 9. Required-change workflow

Record the finding, severity, exact affected material and required change. After editing, recompute hashes and fingerprint, mark the old evidence stale, and obtain a new review bound to the new fingerprint. Never rewrite a completed record to follow changed content.

## 10. Classroom-trial boundary

A teacher decision and a named-context safety decision are prerequisites, but neither completes a trial. The classroom-trial guide defines separate aggregate, privacy-safe evidence. No learner names or identifiers may be committed.

## 11. Prohibited claims

Do not claim teacher approval, universal safety, classroom or publication readiness, customer release, comparative effectiveness, official curriculum completeness, default-course eligibility, live-catalogue completeness or a changed canonical coverage status.

## 12. How to create a completed record

Create a new YAML file inside this review directory, set `template: false`, enter actual reviewer evidence, pin the exact current fingerprint, complete every scope area and decision field, then register the immutable path. Never use PR authorship, review state or merge state as evidence.
