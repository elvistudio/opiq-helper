# Classroom-trial guide: Grade 6 photosynthesis artifact

## 1. Purpose and non-evidence boundary

This guide defines a future fail-closed classroom-trial workflow. No trial is conducted in this pull request. Creating or merging the template is not evidence and creates no readiness claim.

## 2. Prerequisite teacher and safety reviews

Before a trial starts, a current completed teacher review must approve the exact fingerprint and a current local-safety review must approve the named classroom context. Conversational approval, PR approval or merge state cannot satisfy either prerequisite.

## 3. Exact artifact and fingerprint

Artifact: `grade-6-science-photosynthesis`. Route: `grade-6-science`. Fingerprint: `8df9cff3e19c325ba92f931f72c79cf2828a9b03a36fcf80ea19aff430d7db45`. Any material-byte change invalidates earlier review and trial evidence.

## 4. Privacy and aggregate evidence

Use opaque facilitator and group references. Commit only aggregate observations: no learner or facilitator names, emails, contacts, individual grades, diagnostic information, recordings or identifiable media. Perform manual and free-text privacy review before registration.

## 5. Preparing the trial

Pin the approved `indoor_classroom` safety context: organization, exact room, date, maximum group size, minimum adult supervision, accessibility arrangements, emergency process and every condition. Confirm cool LED, dry electrical connections outside the spill tray, 20 cm distance, stable plastic containers and non-release disposal.

## 6. Part 1 observation procedure

Observe the single 45-minute part without helping the method appear more successful. Capture timing, setup, instruction comprehension, practical safety, variable control, crossover execution, observation quality, calculation, evidence/interpretation separation, material usability, accessibility, cleanup and non-release, immediate transfer and method naturalness.

## 7. Part 2 observation procedure

There is no second lesson part in this artifact. Do not add an invented part or import evidence from another artifact. Follow-up activity requires a separately registered design.

## 8. Timing and transition evidence

Record actual duration and whether setup, first 5-minute acclimation, three one-minute intervals, crossover, second acclimation, second set of intervals, calculation and cleanup fit the planned time. A conducted record without actual duration is incomplete.

## 9. Safety, stop conditions and incidents

Stop for water near electrical connections, wet-hand contact with lamp controls, unstable or damaged containers, lamp heating, eye exposure, intentional tasting, plant release risk or another local stop condition. Record aggregate incident and stop counts; safety and effectiveness remain separate claims.

## 10. Findings and required changes

Link each partly-met dimension to an exact minor finding and plan. A major or blocking open finding prevents a positive decision. Paths must remain inside the artifact root, and resolved findings need resolution notes.

## 11. Trial decisions

Only an analysed record may decide `successful`, `successful_with_notes`, `repeat_trial_required` or `unsuccessful`. A positive decision requires current prerequisites, privacy attestations, fingerprint match, complete observations and `safe_to_repeat: true`. One positive trial does not establish comparative effectiveness.

## 12. Fingerprint invalidation

Material changes produce a new fingerprint. An old analysed record stays immutable and historical; it cannot support the changed artifact.

## 13. Registration of an analysed record

Create a new record inside the review root, complete aggregate context and observations, confirm the immutable safety snapshot, set lifecycle to `analysed`, validate privacy, and register its path. Draft or merely conducted records are not completed evidence.
