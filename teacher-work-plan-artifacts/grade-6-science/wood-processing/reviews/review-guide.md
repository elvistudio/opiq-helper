## 6. Finding severity definitions

`note` records context; `minor` requires a bounded correction; `major` blocks a positive decision until resolved; `blocking` prevents use. Positive decisions cannot hide open major or blocking findings.

## 7. Approval rules

A template cannot approve anything. Completed records require real reviewer identity, role, date, full scope, current fingerprint and explicit decision. Named-context approval never transfers automatically to another site, date or group.

## 8. Fingerprint invalidation

Any material-byte change changes a material hash and aggregate fingerprint. Old review evidence then becomes stale and cannot support readiness.

## 9. Required-change workflow

Record each required change, update only the affected material, recompute hashes and fingerprint, and obtain review of the new bytes. Do not rewrite historical completed evidence.

## 10. Classroom-trial boundary
