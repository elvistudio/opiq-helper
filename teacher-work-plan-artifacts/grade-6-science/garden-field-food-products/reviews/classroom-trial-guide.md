# Classroom-trial guide: Grade 6 garden and field food products

## 1. Purpose and non-evidence boundary

No trial is conducted in this pull request. Creating or merging this template is not evidence and does not change readiness.

## 2. Prerequisite teacher and safety reviews

A trial requires current teacher approval plus a current local-safety approval for the exact named indoor classroom, date, group size, supervision and sample conditions. PR state cannot satisfy prerequisites.

## 3. Exact artifact and fingerprint

Artifact: `grade-6-science-garden-field-food-products`. Fingerprint: `999eb50584622bb35dd017a34d7b83536c4face4ebaccd98d12d7768518280ad`.

## 4. Privacy and aggregate evidence

Use opaque group and facilitator references only. Commit no learner names, IDs, contact details, profiles, medical information, recordings or identifiable media. Future analysed evidence must remain aggregate.

## 5. Preparing the trial

Confirm the source cards, opaque sample IDs, no-tasting rule, allergy and cross-contact review, sealed packaging, teacher-only cutting, clean surfaces, accessibility alternatives and disposal plan.

## 6. Part 1 observation procedure

Observe the 45-minute structured comparison for lesson 22. Record timing, transitions, comprehension, safety, source-card use, classifications, calculations, conclusion quality, accessibility, cleanup and Estonian support.

## 7. Data-quality observations

Check whether learners separated direct observation, source-card evidence, calculation, interpretation and limitations. `Unknown` and `uncertain` must remain acceptable.

## 8. Timing and transition evidence

Record actual duration and aggregate transition issues. Do not reconstruct missing events from memory as if observed.

## 9. Safety, stop conditions and incidents

Stop for tasting, an allergy reaction, spill, sharp-object issue, damaged package, hygiene failure or loss of supervision. Safety incidents and stop conditions are separate from learning outcomes.

## 10. Findings and required changes

Link every partly met dimension to an exact minor finding and plan. Open major or blocking findings prevent a positive decision. Affected paths stay inside this artifact root.

## 11. Trial decisions

Only analysed evidence may yield `successful`, `successful_with_notes`, `repeat_trial_required` or `unsuccessful`. A positive trial does not establish comparative effectiveness.

## 12. Fingerprint invalidation

Material changes invalidate prior trial evidence. A future record must match the current fingerprint and current prerequisite reviews.

## 13. Registration of an analysed record

Register only immutable analysed records. Supersession is graph-derived; draft or conducted records do not support readiness, and unrelated active terminals fail closed.

## 14. Readiness truth table

Workflow only: not ready. Pending reviews: not ready. Conducted but unanalysed trial: not ready. A valid active analysed trial can contribute only after all separate guards pass; it cannot alone grant publication or effectiveness claims.

## 15. Prohibited claims

Do not claim learner-level results, universal safety, health effects, comparative effectiveness, direct Opiq evidence or source-gap resolution. Lesson 22 remains missing.
