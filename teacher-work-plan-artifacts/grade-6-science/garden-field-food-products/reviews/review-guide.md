## 5. Local-safety-review procedure

Approval is limited to one named school, exact classroom, date, group size, adult supervision and locally selected samples. Review allergies, cross-contact, hygiene, package integrity, teacher-only cutting, no tasting, disposal, accessibility and stop signals. `indoor_classroom` is the intended delivery category.

## 6. Finding severity definitions

`note` records context; `minor` requires a bounded correction; `major` blocks a positive decision until resolved; `blocking` prevents use. Positive decisions cannot hide open major or blocking findings.

## 7. Approval rules

A template cannot approve anything. Completed records require real reviewer identity, role, date, full scope, current fingerprint and explicit decision. Local safety approval never transfers to another site, date, group or sample set.

## 8. Fingerprint invalidation

Any material-byte change changes a material hash and aggregate fingerprint. Old review evidence then becomes stale and cannot support readiness.

## 9. Required-change workflow

Record each required change, update only the affected material, recompute hashes and fingerprint, and obtain review of the new bytes. Do not rewrite historical completed evidence.

## 10. Classroom-trial boundary

No trial may begin until a current approved teacher record and current named-context safety approval exist. A classroom-trial template is workflow infrastructure, not evidence.

## 11. Prohibited claims

Do not commit learner identities. Do not infer healthiness, allergy safety, sustainability, official curriculum completeness, default-course status, publication readiness or effectiveness. Lesson 22 remains `missing`.

## 12. How to create a completed record
