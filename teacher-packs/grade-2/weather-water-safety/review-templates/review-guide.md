# Grade 2 weather/water pilot review guide

This is a template-only workflow for a module whose four lessons are internally
authored but not release-ready. No review has been recorded.

The reviewer should inspect:

- the standalone Russian explanations and the bounded Estonian support;
- the exact approved task-bank integrations in lesson 2;
- individual evidence before any shared board;
- window-first observation and adult-supervised outdoor boundaries;
- separate subject and Estonian-language criteria;
- the exact one-sentence productive contract `Täna on ___ ilm.` with only
  `vihmane`, `tuuline`, `pilvine`, or `päikeseline` in the blank;
- the lesson 3 canonical human-studies route without using it as PE proof;
- the author-created PE missing-route role with no route, Opiq record, URL or
  `md_path`;
- the exact pending task 09/10 projections, fingerprints and null review identity;
- individual task 09/10 attempts before pair discussion and teacher answers;
- dry classroom simulation with no real water or rescue practice;
- separate human-studies, PE and Estonian evidence, with
  `Ma kutsun täiskasvanu.` as the only required productive lesson 3 sentence;
- the exact science-only lesson 4 route and guided-inquiry official outcome;
- exact pending task 11/12 projections, fingerprints and null review identity;
- task 11 individual two-sentence frame and personal code before group assembly;
- criteria-based evaluation of task 11 without mandatory wording;
- task 12 closed first attempt with 09:00 = 7 °C, 13:00 = 14 °C, the false
  `В 13:00 было холоднее`, blank `Kell 13 on ____.` frame and
  `soojem | külmem` bank;
- exactly three numbered task 12 responses and no answer before first attempt;
- `Kell 13 on soojem.` as the only required productive lesson 4 sentence;
- separate individual contribution, exit ticket and reflection evidence that
  the shared report cannot replace;
- four authored lessons, zero planned slots, while all reviews, trials,
  companion verification, readiness and effectiveness claims remain blocked.

A completed review must be stored as a separate record. This guide does not
grant teacher approval, classroom readiness, home readiness, publication
readiness, or effectiveness.
